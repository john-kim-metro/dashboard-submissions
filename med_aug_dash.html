Data source: MetroDermatologyBIData semantic model (Power BI), live query 2026-09-10; model last refreshed 2026-09-10 09:39 AM ET. Volume, no-shows and cancellations come from fact_Appointment; medical scope is the MED - facility prefix (6 active locations). Cancellations are counted from appointment_status = "CANCELLED", not derived by subtraction. NS% = NS / (NS + Completed); cancellations are excluded from that denominator. September 2026 is excluded throughout as a partial month. Three cosmetic-typed appointments booked at medical locations were left in the demand total (0 completed, immaterial). System ODE = 23.5 (21 weekdays + 5 Saturdays × 0.5), no practice-wide closure in August. Single-site closures are taken from metroapp_shared.schedule_holiday and verified against daily visit counts. Chart text is rendered at a minimum 2× pixel density and 11px so it stays sharp in print, PDF export and screenshots. Prepared by Darell Dado, Metro Dermatology.

  • Conversion rate is the headline advantage. Switching cost is the hidden factor

Our website, location pages, and conversion funnels are already optimized around ZocDoc's BD flow. Re-platforming to NexHealth or another vendor would mean weeks of patients falling through the cracks while we rebuild — a real cost not captured in the headline price comparison.

One-year commitment, not multi-year

A single-year commitment preserves optionality. We continue to monitor BD's contribution against the $30,000 cost and can revisit alternatives in 2027 with a full year of clean data — including Darell's EHR-to-ZocDoc match project, which will let us measure true patient LTV by channel rather than relying on booking volume alone.

Proposed next steps

  1. Execute one-year agreement before trial period expires.
  2. Set quarterly review cadence to track BD conversion, new patient share, and Marketplace synergy.

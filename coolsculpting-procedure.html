What is CoolSculpting®

A clinically proven way to reduce stubborn fat — without surgery, needles, or downtime.

CoolSculpting® uses precisely controlled cooling to target and eliminate fat cells beneath the skin. Once those cells are destroyed, your body naturally clears them away over the following weeks. The result is a more sculpted contour — visible, gradual, and lasting.

FDA-cleared technology

Cleared for the abdomen, flanks, thighs, upper arms, bra fat, back, banana roll, and chin/jawline. Over 11 million treatments performed worldwide.

No surgery, no downtime

No incisions, anesthesia, or recovery. Most patients return to work, the gym, and daily activities the same day as treatment.

Permanent results

The fat cells destroyed during treatment do not regenerate. With a stable weight, the contoured results you see at three months are the results you keep.

Is it right for you?

Designed for the last 20–30 pounds of stubborn fat.

CoolSculpting® is a body contouring treatment, not a weight-loss procedure. It works best for patients near their goal weight who have specific areas resistant to diet and exercise.

You're likely a great candidate if…

  • You're within 20–30 pounds of your goal weight
  • You have specific areas of pinchable fat that won't respond to exercise
  • You've reached a stable weight after a fitness journey or GLP-1 weight loss
  • You want gradual, natural-looking results without surgery or downtime

CoolSculpting may not be right if…

  • You're looking for significant weight loss (consider medical weight management first)
  • You have significant skin laxity in the treatment area (skin tightening may be a better option)
  • You're pregnant or have certain cold-sensitivity conditions
  • You've had recent surgery in the treatment area

Common questions

Frequently asked.

What's the difference between a "cycle" and a "session"?
A cycle is one placement of the CoolSculpting® applicator on one spot — about 35–45 minutes of treatment. A session is your appointment, which may include multiple cycles back-to-back. Most treatment areas need 2–4 cycles per session, and most patients complete 1–2 sessions per area.
Does it hurt?
Most patients describe the first 5–10 minutes as intense cold with a pulling sensation from the suction. After that, the area numbs and many patients read, scroll, or even nap during the cycle. The post-treatment massage can be briefly uncomfortable. You may feel mild soreness, like a deep bruise, for a few days afterward.
How many sessions will I need?
Most patients need 1–3 sessions per treatment area, spaced 6–8 weeks apart. Smaller areas like the chin often complete in one session; larger areas like the abdomen typically benefit from two. We will design a specific plan during your free consultation.
What areas of the body can be treated?
CoolSculpting® is FDA-cleared for nine areas: abdomen (upper and lower), flanks ("love handles"), inner and outer thighs, upper arms, the submental area (under the chin), the submandibular area (jawline), bra fat (side and back), and the banana roll (under the buttocks). It is precise enough to target small areas like the chin, and powerful enough to address larger areas like the abdomen and thighs.
When will I see results?
First visible changes appear at 3–4 weeks. Most patients see noticeable results between 1–3 months. Full results emerge at 3–6 months as your body's lymphatic system completes clearing the destroyed fat cells.
Are the results permanent?
Yes — the fat cells destroyed by CoolSculpting® do not regenerate. However, the remaining fat cells in the area can still enlarge with significant weight gain. Maintaining a stable weight protects your results long-term.

Is there any downtime?
No. You can return to work, exercise, and normal activities the same day. Some patients experience temporary redness, swelling, tingling, or numbness in the treated area, all of which typically resolve within 1–2 weeks.

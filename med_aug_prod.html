Ideal quadrant
Lee (6.99 / 8.2%), Park (6.65 / 11.5%), Huh (6.44 / 13.5%), Hu (6.39 / 12.5%), Sun (6.14 / 13.9%), Kim (5.45 / 8.9%)

Six providers clear the 5.12 production average on a no-show rate under 15%. This is the scheduling-density benchmark: Lee runs the system’s highest throughput on its lowest no-show rate, and Kim reaches 5.45 on 8.9% — adherence, not hours, is what separates this group. Three more — Park H. (6.74), Hussain (5.86) and Li (5.41) — clear the average on green but run no-show at 17–21%, so they sit just outside this quadrant.
Leaky panels — no-show ≥ 25%
Ortiz (6.64 / 31.2%), Shin (6.63 / 28.5%), Huang (5.03 / 25.5%), Kwak (4.86 / 27.2%), Sit (4.37 / 25.7%)

Between them these carry the largest recoverable volume in the system. Two of the three highest-VPH providers sit here, so this is a conversion problem, not a capacity one.
Below benchmark
Yu (4.71 / 17.1%), Garcia (3.37 / 19.3%)

Both are ramp cases rather than idle capacity — Garcia is fifteen months in and Yu carries genuine volume. Providers below the average who also run no-show at or above 25% are counted in the leaky group, not here. Espinal (3.50 / 28.0%) is plotted in purple and held out of all three groups while she is still ramping — she is the only provider in the system both below the average and above the 25% no-show line.

Fastest ramp — CH (Huang), +0.239/mo, R² 0.86
The fastest and most linear ramp in the cohort: 0.93 in March ’25 to a 5.91 peak in May ’26. August’s 4.89 recovers on July’s 4.80 but is a third month below the 5.12 Month‑18 mark, which she is now due to hold. She is comfortably clear of the 4.5 Month‑12 target and the trend line remains the cohort’s cleanest fit.
Watch this month — PS (Sit), 4.80 → 4.15
Three consecutive monthly falls from the May peak, back under the 4.5 Month‑12 milestone she cleared in April. Unlike July, this is not a denominator effect: system templated hours rose 1.4% in August while her VPH fell 3.0%, so the visits are genuinely not there. Slope is still positive at +0.179/mo. Worth a schedule and panel review before the September cycle.
Still short — GG (Garcia), +0.100/mo
August’s 3.34 recovers on July’s 3.23 but stays under the 3.5 Month‑6 target she first cleared in June at 3.65. The slope is the cohort’s shallowest; on that trend the 4.5 Month‑12 mark is roughly a year away. Fifteen months in, this is the one ramp in the cohort that has not converged — the intervention flag should stay on.

Net collection rate = payments ÷ collectible (net) revenue. Each month the window moves forward by one month and keeps only service months that are at least 90 days old, so payments are substantially complete. The three months are pooled by dollars rather than averaging the monthly percentages. Recent months can still gain about 0.5–1 point as late payments post, so the figure is slightly conservative.
Cost to collect = RCM cost ÷ collections for the same months. RCM cost covers employee payroll, estimated HVA pay and consulting; software, clearinghouse fees and overhead aren’t included yet, so the true figure is somewhat higher.
Reimbursement rate isn’t in the current data.

Quick answers

What's a "cycle"?
A cycle is one placement of the CoolSculpting® applicator on one spot — about 35 to 45 minutes of treatment. Most areas need multiple cycles for full coverage. Your provider will recommend the right number during your free consultation.
Does this offer apply to any treatment area?
Yes — the Buy 3 Get 1 Free offer applies to any treatment plan using the included applicators (Cool Advance, Cool Core, Cool Curve, or Cool Smooth). The Cool Max applicator is excluded from this promotion.
When do I need to book by?
Treatments must be scheduled and completed between May 26 and July 31. We recommend booking your free consultation as soon as possible to secure your preferred time slot during the promotion window.
Can I combine this with other offers?
This promotion cannot be combined with other offers or discounts. We do offer financing through CareCredit if you'd like to spread the payment out.

Questions & Answers

Everything you need to know.

What's the difference between the Acne Program and the Red Acne Scar Program?
The Acne Program is designed for active breakouts — targeting the bacteria, inflammation, and clogged pores that cause ongoing acne. The Red Acne Scar Program is for people whose acne has cleared but who are left with persistent redness, discoloration, and post-acne marks on the skin. Both programs use clinical-grade treatments, but the specific combination and visit spacing are tailored to each condition.
Can I do both programs at the same time?
That depends on your skin's current condition. Some patients with both active acne and lingering scars may benefit from a sequential or overlapping approach. Your provider will assess your skin during the free consultation and recommend the best plan for you.
How quickly will I see results?
Most patients begin noticing improvement after the first 1–2 visits. Because we combine multiple treatments per visit — including laser therapy, light therapy, and chemical peels — results build progressively with each session. Significant improvement is typically visible by the end of the program.
What does Vbeam® laser do for acne and acne scars?
Vbeam® is a pulsed dye laser that precisely targets red pigment in the skin. For active acne, it reduces inflammation and kills acne-causing bacteria. For red acne scars, it breaks down the discolored blood vessels that create persistent redness — without damaging the surrounding skin.
Is there any downtime after treatments?
Most treatments in these programs have minimal to no downtime. Some patients experience mild redness or sensitivity for 24–48 hours following laser or peel treatments. Your provider will give you specific post-care instructions after each visit.
How do I get started?
Book a free consultation at any of our locations — online or by calling us directly. Your provider will review your skin, discuss your goals, and confirm which program is right for you. There's no obligation and no pressure.

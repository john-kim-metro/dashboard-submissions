02
Promotion Performance

Q1 2026 opened with three distinct promotions, each designed to reach a different patient need and season. Together they pushed over 125,000 messages and generated 5,264 scans and 264 direct calls — a strong signal of engaged patient response across every channel. While Valentine's Day set the tone with filler awareness, the 30th Anniversary became the quarter's revenue engine, and the Jeuveau & Letybo Event closed the period with its single-day lead performance.

Valentine's Day Promotion

Jan 19 – Feb 14, 2026  ·  Restylane & RHA Fillers

Completed

The quarter opened with a Valentine's Day filler promotion targeting patients interested in Restylane and RHA. Running through the holiday weekend, the campaign leaned entirely on SMS and email outreach — with no Meta Ad support — making its 707 scans and 53 calls a testament to the strength of our existing patient database. Restylane revenue grew +8.4% year-over-year, with variant SKUs like Defyne and Lyft showing the strongest gains, suggesting patients are becoming more treatment-specific in their requests.

30th Anniversary Promotion

Mar 2 – May 10, 2026  ·  Gift Card + Ultherapy PRIME

Completed

Metro Dermatology's 30th Anniversary was the quarter's most ambitious campaign — running for 10 weeks with two distinct Meta Ad flights, a gift card promotion tied to a milestone moment, and the debut of Ultherapy PRIME. The first flight introduced PRIME to existing patients; the second pivoted to Mother's Day gifting and delivered a dramatic improvement in Meta Ad efficiency, cutting cost-per-click from $3.20 to $0.44. Backed by the new ability for patients to share and gift their gift cards, the campaign reached its broadest audience of the quarter — 60,567 combined SMS and email contacts.

💡Gift Card Insight — Shareable Gift Cards
In 2026, Metro Dermatology gift cards became shareable — patients can purchase them as gifts for others, not just for personal use. Combined with a longer promotion window (Mar–May 2026 vs Apr–May 2025 — nearly double the duration), these two factors together drove a +45.0% revenue increase (+$123,104). The extended timeline gave more patients the opportunity to discover and act on the promotion, while the shareable format encouraged higher-value purchases as gifters bought on behalf of loved ones — particularly amplified by the Mother's Day angle of the 2nd Meta Ad flight.

Jeuveau & Letybo Special Event

Apr 18, 2026  ·  One-day event (2–5 PM)  ·  Flushing & Fort Lee

Completed

The Jeuveau & Letybo Special Event was a focused, single-day activation at two of our highest-volume locations — Flushing and Fort Lee. What it lacked in duration it made up for in precision: a targeted Meta Ad campaign leading up to the event generated 68,808 views and 991 landing page views, warming the audience before the day arrived. The result was the quarter's highest single-promotion scan total at 2,663 — with Social Media scans (991) nearly matching SMS scans (1,610), reflecting strong organic reach on top of the paid push. It was also the introduction of Letybo to our patient base, with revenue from that treatment set to appear in May reporting.

Cosmetic Inquiry Form — January to April 2026
📋

The cosmetic inquiry form captured an additional layer of leads beyond SMS, email, and GMB — reflecting patients who engaged directly through the website. With 244 submissions and a 27.0% completion rate, this channel represents high-intent prospects actively seeking cosmetic services.

Beyond outbound campaigns, the cosmetic inquiry form served as a continuous inbound lead channel throughout the quarter. Of the 2,474 patients who viewed the form, 905 started filling it out — a 36.6% engagement rate that speaks to genuine intent. Of those, 244 completed and submitted their inquiry, averaging just 2 minutes and 10 seconds to complete. The top service of interest was Laser Treatments (116 responses), followed by Skin Rejuvenation/Facials (71) and Neuromodulators (56) — data that directly informs how we position upcoming promotions like the CoolSculpting and summer campaigns.

Services of Interest — Cosmetic Inquiry Form
📊

248 out of 267 people answered this question. Laser Treatments ranked #1 with 116 responses — nearly double the next category. Neuromodulators and Acne & Acne Scarring tied closely at 56 and 55 respectively.

The services of interest data offers a direct window into what our cosmetic patients are thinking about right now. Laser Treatments dominate at 116 responses — nearly twice any other category — which aligns with the strong performance of V-Beam (+28.9% patient growth YoY) in our patient flow data. Skin Rejuvenation and Neuromodulators round out the top three, confirming that non-invasive, results-driven treatments remain the core demand driver. Notably, Skin Tightening (30 responses) — which includes Ultherapy — shows a steady patient base that the 30th Anniversary promotion was well-positioned to serve. This data should inform Q2 and Q3 creative direction, particularly for the upcoming CoolSculpting, Acne Summer, and FIFA promotions.

05
Key Observations

Q1 2026 demonstrated that Metro Dermatology's marketing reach is both broad and efficient. With a combined spend of just $1,057.36 in Meta Ads, the team generated over 5,000 trackable scans and 264 direct calls. The quarter validated three important trends: one-day events can outperform multi-week campaigns in raw lead volume; shareable gift cards combined with extended promotion windows significantly lift revenue; and the cosmetic inquiry form is an underutilized but high-intent channel that deserves deeper integration into future campaigns.

💡Gift Card Insight — Shareable Gift Cards
In 2026, Metro Dermatology gift cards became shareable — patients can purchase them as gifts for others, not just for personal use. This drove a +45.0% revenue increase (+$123,104) by encouraging higher purchase amounts and expanding the buyer pool. The Mother's Day focus of the 2nd Meta Ad flight directly leveraged this, prompting gifters to purchase on behalf of loved ones.
